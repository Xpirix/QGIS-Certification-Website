Courses

A course is run at a training center by a course convener for a particular course. When creating a course, you can add attendees to the course. Attendees do not need to register on the QGIS Certification web platform.

Each course also says how people join it: in person, online or hybrid. Hybrid means some people join in person and others join online. We show this on the course page, in "Courses coming soon" on the home page and in the course feeds.
